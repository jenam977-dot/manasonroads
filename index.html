About

The rider behind the films.

MANASONROADS is a cinematic motorcycle travel project.

I ride the road, film the journey and turn the places I discover into stories.

No destination is just a pin on a map. The road, the people, the unexpected moments — everything between the start and the finish is part of the story.

I'm Manas. I ride solo, film solo, and cut every journey into a documentary.
