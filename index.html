PLACES TO VISIT

The stops that shape the route.

DeomaliMust visit

Odisha's highest peak — mountain roads, grass plateaus and panoramic views.

Duduma WaterfallMust visit

A major waterfall in the Machkund region, reached through winding rural roads.

Sabar Srikhetra, KoraputMust visit

A hilltop Jagannath temple complex tied to the region's indigenous cultural heritage.

Kolab ReservoirOptional

Planned as an evening stop near Koraput.

Gupteswar Limestone CavesOptional

A possible detour — the plan keeps it optional to avoid excessive additional riding.

About

The rider behind the films.

MANASONROADS is a cinematic motorcycle travel project.

I ride the road, film the journey and turn the places I discover into stories.

No destination is just a pin on a map. The road, the people, the unexpected moments — everything between the start and the finish is part of the story.

I'm Manas. I ride solo, film solo, and cut every journey into a documentary.
